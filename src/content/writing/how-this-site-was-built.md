## The idea

Real-world systems are chaos. The home page tries to show that in motion: scattered points that settle into a lattice, then four chapters where a messy world becomes legible.

## How it was made

I worked with coding agents end to end: the brief, the scenes, the copy edits and the render checks. Every chapter is a small scene on one WebGL canvas, and the page moves one step at a time so each animation gets its moment.
