These are questions, not products. I do not start one because the topic is circulating.

Trust. What an AI answer is allowed to claim, and how a person can tell.

Connectors. The gap where an agent still cannot reach the trade, especially commerce in India.

Evidence. A record of an agent’s action, so a later person can see why it happened.

Attribution. When an agent sends the order, the merchant still has to know it counted.

Handshake. Agent to agent: identity, authorization, and evidence, or it is not a transaction.
