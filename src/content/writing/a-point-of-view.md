I build from a point of view I already hold, not because something is trending. If I cannot say who is stuck, and why, it is not a project.

Part Index came from that. Finding an auto part in India is still a conversation across a counter. The names are messy, and people do not type them the way a catalogue expects. Search should survive that. It lives at [whileyousleep.xyz](https://whileyousleep.xyz). The studio is one person.

The [Gupshup MCP server](https://github.com/ManmohanBuildsProducts/gupshup-mcp) is the same kind of decision, smaller. An agent that cannot reach someone on WhatsApp or SMS is not finished, so I gave it the pipe.

A Mac Mini at home runs local models. That stays a workshop. I do not call an experiment a product.
