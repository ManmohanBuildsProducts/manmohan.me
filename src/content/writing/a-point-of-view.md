I build from a point of view I already hold, not because something is trending. If I cannot say who is stuck, and why, it is not a project.

Part Index came from that. Finding an auto part in India is still a conversation across a counter. People mix Hindi and English, transliterate, misspell, ask by the symptom, and use a brand name as the generic. Search has to survive that. It lives at [whileyousleep.xyz](https://whileyousleep.xyz). The studio is one person, and the project is a prototype. I am not claiming customers.

The [Gupshup MCP server](https://github.com/ManmohanBuildsProducts/gupshup-mcp) is the same kind of decision, smaller. An agent that cannot reach someone on WhatsApp or SMS is not finished. Logging is redacted. Retries are explicit.

A Mac Mini at home runs local models. A fast one first, a larger or cloud model when a check fails. That stays a workshop. I do not call an experiment a product, and I do not publish speeds.
